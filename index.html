About Me

I am an enterprise infrastructure professional with 15+ years of experience across IT infrastructure, virtualization, platform operations and infrastructure transformation. My professional journey began with enterprise systems and Windows infrastructure, progressed into VMware technologies—including ESXi, vCenter and vSphere—and expanded into complex virtualization environments and modern software-defined data center platforms. Today, my primary focus is VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) and enterprise infrastructure. I bring hands-on experience across virtualization platforms, infrastructure operations, migrations, automation and technical problem-solving in enterprise environments.

Alongside my VMware expertise, I work with AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, using tools such as Terraform, Ansible and scripting to support infrastructure automation and modernization. My experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD and observability further complements my infrastructure background. I combine established enterprise infrastructure knowledge with modern automation and cloud capabilities to help build reliable, scalable and operationally efficient platforms.
